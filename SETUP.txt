KHazaana customer notification package

FILES
- index.html = Admin Panel with customer status notification calls
- track-order.html = Customer tracking page with push notification registration
- firebase-messaging-sw.js = Firebase background notification service worker
- server.js = Backend with customer registration + status notification endpoints

DEPLOYMENT
1. Replace your live Admin Panel with index.html.
2. Replace your live track-order.html with track-order.html.
3. Upload firebase-messaging-sw.js beside track-order.html on the customer HTTPS site.
4. Deploy server.js to Render.
5. In Render, keep FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT and add: CUSTOMER_TRACKING_BASE_URL=https://helpkhazaana-eng.github.io/CNC-MENU
6. Customer must open the tracking page over HTTPS and allow notifications. They do not need to keep the site open after permission is granted.
7. Admin status buttons now call /notify-customer-status after updating Firestore.
